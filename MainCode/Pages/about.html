Who we are

About Lumin Death

A small label making hard-wearing clothing in short runs, sold direct.

Our story

Started in a spare room

Lumin Death began as a handful of screen-printed tees made for friends. People kept asking where they could buy them, so we started making more — carefully, and never more than we could finish properly.

Today we work with one small factory and a short list of suppliers we know by name. Runs are deliberately limited: when a piece sells out it usually stays out, and anything that comes back does so because enough people asked.

How we make things

Heavyweight cotton for the knits, coated technical fabrics for the shells, and hardware chosen because it survives daily use. Every sample is worn for a few weeks before it goes into production — if a seam gives out, the pattern gets redrawn.

The basics

What you can expect

Made in small runs

Most pieces are produced fewer than two hundred at a time, so nothing feels mass-market and quality stays consistent.

Shipped within 48 hours

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day leave the same week, tracked, with worldwide delivery available.

30 day returns

If the fit is not right, send it back unworn within thirty days and we will refund it in full.
